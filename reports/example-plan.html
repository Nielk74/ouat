Risks

important riskMake retry eligibility explicit

Repeating an unsafe operation

When: An operation is misclassified as safe after its first attempt performed a side effect.

Consequence: A retry can repeat the side effect.

Mitigation: Confirm each operation with its upstream contract; leave unknown operations ineligible.

important riskDefine limits and preserve the external contract

Amplifying an upstream outage

When: Concurrent requests retry the same transient failure.

Consequence: Extra calls can increase load on an unhealthy upstream.

Mitigation: Keep the attempt cap and jitter; validate upstream tolerance under load.
