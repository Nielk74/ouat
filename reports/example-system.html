Context

Baseline

Every five minutes, cron invokes the API, which builds and saves the export before replying.

Scope

Hypothetical proposal; files, snippets, infrastructure, and products are illustrative.

Constraints

  • Publish durably before accepting; acknowledge after saving.
  • Bound concurrency and preserve job IDs across deliveries.

Assumptions / unknowns

  • The broker supports durable delivery and results have unique job IDs.
  • The consume wrapper acknowledges after its callback succeeds.

05

Risks

important riskConsume work in a bounded thread pool

A job can be delivered more than once

When: A consumer stops after saving but before acknowledging.

Consequence: Redelivery can repeat work and side effects.

Mitigation: Preserve job IDs, upsert results, and make other effects idempotent.

important riskDeploy the consumer independently

Jobs accumulate without a healthy consumer

When: The worker is stopped or its queue binding is wrong.

Consequence: Accepted jobs wait for completion.

Mitigation: Verify consumption after deployment; monitor queue age and worker health.
