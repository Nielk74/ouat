Local brand artwork for product identification in technical review reports.

The catalog.json file is the inventory and provenance record. Each entry names
the upstream asset, revision, official source, known license, brand guidelines,
and any archived-mark note. Standalone SVG files retain downloaded artwork.
The report renderer removes editor metadata and unsafe SVG features, namespaces
internal IDs, and applies the recorded brand color to monochrome Simple Icons
marks. It does not change their path geometry or recolor them by change status.

Simple Icons project contributions are CC0; individual icon licenses and
trademark terms may differ. See the included upstream license and disclaimer.
Unspecified license metadata is not permission or a claim that an asset is CC0.
Archived marks from 11.15.0 are labeled in the catalog and visual gallery.
